# BUG-01 – Cart Quantity Increase/Decrease Option

## Description
The Cart displays the product quantity as 1, but there is no option to increase or decrease the quantity.

## Module
Cart

## Severity
Medium

## Status
Open

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the e-commerce website.
2. Add a product to the Cart.
3. Open the Cart.
4. Check the Quantity section.
5. Try to increase or decrease the quantity.

## Expected Result
The Cart should provide an option such as +/− buttons to increase or decrease the product quantity.

## Actual Result
The product quantity is displayed as 1, but no option is available to increase or decrease the quantity.
